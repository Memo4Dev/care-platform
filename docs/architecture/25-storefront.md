# Storefront / E-Commerce Context

## Owns
- Store
- StoreProduct publication/presentation
- CustomDomain
- Store branding/settings

## Rules
- Each Organization has its own Store.
- Organization may customize logo/colors/domain/products/categories.
- Master product stays in Catalog.
- Online price comes from Pricing.
- Online stock comes from Inventory.
- Online Cart comes from Cart Context.
- Online Orders come from Orders Context.
- Store can be suspended without disabling POS.
