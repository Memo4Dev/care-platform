# Responsive pattern

Desktop is the primary ERP workspace, but every workflow must remain usable on narrow viewports.

- 900px: collapse the rail; convert the product form to one column; metrics become two columns.
- 620px: use a horizontal navigation row; stack page actions and filters; metrics and panels become one column.
- Tables retain their data structure and scroll horizontally rather than shrinking numeric columns.
- Keep controls at least 40px high and avoid horizontal page overflow.
